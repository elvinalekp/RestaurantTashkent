Welcome

Uzbek cooking is made for sharing.

Big platters, warm bread and plenty of tea: the cuisine of Uzbekistan is built around the table. Restaurant Tashkent brings that spirit to Kettering for dine-in, takeout and delivery.

Everything here is halal, so families and friends can order with confidence.
